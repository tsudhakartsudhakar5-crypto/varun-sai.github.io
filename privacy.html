Privacy Policy

How this website handles information you choose to share.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy covers the VARUN SAI author website (tsudhakartsudhakar5-crypto.github.io/varun-sai.github.io/). It explains, in plain language, what information the site handles when you browse, send a message, share feedback or vote in a poll, and what you can do about it.

Legal status: India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 are being brought into force in phases, and most duties for organisations start in May 2027. We are preparing for them now. This is a plain-language notice, not legal advice.

1. Who is responsible

The website is operated by Mrs. Thipparapu Sujatha on behalf of the author, Varun Sai. For privacy questions, requests and complaints write to tsudhakartsudhakar5@gmail.com or use Your Data Rights. The VARUN SAI BOOKSTORE is a separate website with its own privacy notice and its own database.

2. Browsing

You can read the whole website without an account and without giving us any personal details. To work, the site connects to services that can see technical details such as your IP address, device and browser type:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Supabase holds the articles, quotes, announcements and polls that the pages load.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts, so your browser contacts Google.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and the site sets no cookies of its own. The share buttons are ordinary links: nothing is sent to WhatsApp, X or Facebook unless you tap one.

3. Information you choose to send

  • Contact form: name, email, subject and message. We use them only to read and answer your message. They are stored in our Supabase database and are meant to be read only by the site administrators.
  • Reader feedback: name, message and an optional email address. We review feedback first. If approved, your name and message appear on the Reader’s Corner page. Your email is not shown.
  • Polls: your choice, plus a random code that your browser creates and keeps in its local storage so each browser can vote once per poll. We do not ask for your name or email to vote. We do not keep the code itself: the database stores only a one-way scrambled version (a salted hash) of it, together with your choice. It is not your identity, but it is still an identifier, so we call poll data pseudonymous rather than anonymous. Because we cannot tell whose code it is, we cannot find or remove one person’s vote; clearing your browser data removes the code from your device.
  • Browser storage: besides the poll code, your browser remembers which polls you have voted in. The private author panel keeps an administrator’s sign-in session in local storage. Readers have no accounts.

The tick-box on the forms is a reminder that the forms are for adults. It is not stored and is not proof of age or consent.

4. Why we use it

We use your information to answer messages, review and show approved feedback, run polls, prevent spam and repeat votes, and keep the website secure. We do not sell or rent personal information. We may disclose information if a court or authority legally requires it.

5. Children

Children are welcome to read and enjoy every public page. The contact and feedback forms are for people aged 18 and over; if you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to write for you. We do not knowingly accept personal data from children, and we have not yet set up the verified parental-consent process that the law will require before we could. A tick-box cannot prove age or parental consent. We do not track, profile or target advertising at anyone, children included. If you think a child has sent us personal data, email tsudhakartsudhakar5@gmail.com and we will delete it.

6. Service providers and links

GitHub, Supabase and Google process data under their own terms and may use servers outside India. Links to retailers (such as Amazon or Pothi), social networks or other sites take you away from this website, and their privacy policies apply.

7. How long we keep it

  • Contact messages: until answered, then up to 12 months unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Feedback: while it is published, or until you ask for removal. Feedback we do not publish is deleted within 12 months.
  • Poll choices and codes: kept with the poll results (see section 3 about removal).
  • Records of privacy requests: up to 3 years.
  • The law may require some records or technical logs to be kept for a minimum time, for example a one-year minimum under the DPDP Rules for some logs. Those are kept only as long as required.

8. Keeping data safe

We use secure (HTTPS) connections, database access rules and restricted administrator sign-in, and we keep secret keys out of the public website files. We are a small operation and no online service can promise complete security. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a summary of the personal data we hold about you; to correct, complete or update it; to erase it; or to withdraw a consent you gave. You can also raise a grievance, and nominate another person to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act for yourself. Use Your Data Rights or email tsudhakartsudhakar5@gmail.com from the address you used with us, so we can check it is you. We will reply as quickly as we can and always within 90 days, the longest time the DPDP Rules allow for a grievance.

10. Complaints and changes

Please raise any concern with us first. If we do not resolve it, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is receiving complaints; the Act expects you to try our grievance process first. If we change this policy we will update the date above.

This notice describes the website as reviewed on 14 November 2026. It has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.
