Privacy Policy

How this website handles information you choose to share.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy applies to the VARUN SAI author website at tsudhakartsudhakar5-crypto.github.io/varun-sai.github.io/. It explains what information is handled when you browse the site, send a message, share reader feedback or vote in a poll.

Important: This is a plain-language privacy notice, not legal advice or a certification of compliance. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 have phased commencement. The website is being prepared with privacy in mind; the operator should review the requirements and implementation with a qualified professional.

1. Who operates this website

The website is operated by the family/author team supporting Varun Sai. For privacy questions or requests, contact tsudhakartsudhakar5@gmail.com. The VARUN SAI BOOKSTORE is a separate website and has its own privacy notice.

2. Browsing the website

You can browse the public pages without creating an account. The website is hosted using GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. The site also connects to a separate Supabase project to load published articles, polls, quotes, announcements and other site content. Those providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device details and request logs to deliver and protect their services.

The site does not intentionally use advertising, behavioural advertising or third-party analytics. It does not create its own advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser may store limited local data for site functions, as described below.

3. Information you choose to send

  • Contact form: name, email address, subject and message. These are sent to the site's Supabase project so the authorised site administrator can read and respond.
  • Reader feedback: name and message, plus an optional email address. Feedback is reviewed before publication. If approved, the name and message may appear publicly on the Reader's Corner page. The optional email address is not intended to be displayed publicly.
  • Poll participation: the poll choice and a randomly generated browser token are sent to the poll service to help enforce one vote per browser per poll. The voting code does not ask for your name or email address as part of the vote. The token is stored in your browser's local storage. This is pseudonymous voting data, not a promise of absolute anonymity; the underlying database and administrator permissions must also be configured to prevent access to identifying technical data.
  • Administrator sign-in: the private author panel uses Supabase authentication. Session tokens may be stored in the administrator's browser local storage. This is for authorised administration, not a public reader account.

4. Why information is used

Information is used to respond to contact messages, review and moderate reader feedback, display approved feedback, operate polls, prevent repeat or abusive submissions, and maintain the website's security and administration. We do not sell or rent personal information.

5. Children and young visitors

Children may browse public pages. The contact and feedback forms are not designed as child accounts or child services. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact the site on your behalf rather than submitting your own personal information. The current website does not implement a verified parental-consent mechanism. A checkbox or a statement alone would not prove a parent's identity or consent. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so we can review and address it.

6. Service providers and external links

GitHub Pages provides hosting, Google Fonts provides typography, and Supabase provides database, API and authentication services for the author website. These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies, and their infrastructure may be located outside India. Links to retailers, social networks or other external websites take you away from this site; their own privacy policies apply. The bookstore uses a separate Supabase project and is not covered by this policy.

7. Storage and retention

Contact messages and reader feedback are stored in the author website's Supabase project. Poll records and the browser token are handled by the poll feature. The exact database backup, log and deletion-retention periods depend partly on the service configuration and have not been independently verified as part of this website-file review. We aim to keep information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected, subject to security, backup and legal requirements. You may request review or deletion using the Your Data Rights page.

8. Security

The website uses HTTPS and a Supabase publishable key in its public configuration. A publishable key is designed to be public; it is not an administrator secret. Database access must be protected by correctly configured Row Level Security (RLS), restricted database functions and administrator permissions. We have reviewed the front-end files, but cannot verify the live database policies, server-side functions, backups or provider logs from these files alone. No internet service can guarantee perfect security.

9. Your choices and requests

You may ask what personal information is held about you, request correction or deletion, withdraw consent where applicable, or raise a privacy concern. Use Your Data Rights or email tsudhakartsudhakar5@gmail.com. Please do not email passwords, government identity numbers or other highly sensitive information. We may need reasonable information to verify a request before acting on it.

10. Changes and complaints

This policy may be updated when the website's features or legal requirements change. The date above will be revised when it is updated. You can first raise a concern using the contact details above. Any statutory complaint route available under applicable law remains unaffected.

This notice describes the front-end website features reviewed on 13 November 2026. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer, and it does not establish that all legal or technical compliance steps have been completed.
