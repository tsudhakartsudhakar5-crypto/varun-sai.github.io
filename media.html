Short biography

Extended biography

Varun Sai is a young author and writer whose work centres on self-awareness, discipline, learning and creativity. His guiding belief is that quality defeats quantity, and that one should master oneself before trying to change the world. He is the author of The Time Changes, a philosophical and motivational book about awareness, courage, action, resilience and personal transformation, and of Learn It. Build It. Master It., a beginner's guide to learning technology and building real projects with AI. He has also released The Unknown Signal, a fairy tale offered free to readers. Outside writing, he enjoys cricket and chess.
