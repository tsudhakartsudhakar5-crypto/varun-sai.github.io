# VARUN SAI Author Website
## Privacy Policy — Draft for Review

**Version:** 1.0  
**Prepared:** 11 November 2026  
**Website:** https://tsudhakartsudhakar5-crypto.github.io/varun-sai.github.io/

### 1. About this policy
This policy explains how information may be collected and used when visitors browse the VARUN SAI author website, contact the operator, submit Reader's Corner feedback or participate in polls. The site provides public information about the author, books, writing, announcements and related content.

The website is operated with parent/guardian involvement. Privacy questions and requests may be sent to **tsudhakartsudhakar5@gmail.com**.

### 2. Information collected
Depending on the feature used, the website may process:

- **Contact form:** name, email address, subject and message.
- **Reader's Corner:** name, message and optional email address.
- **Polls:** poll and option identifiers, a browser-generated client token and a protected hash derived from that token for duplicate-vote prevention.
- **Technical information:** information processed by the hosting, backend, security and browser services, such as request metadata, timestamps, device/browser information or security logs, where those providers collect it.

Visitors can browse public pages without creating an account. No visitor registration or reader-account feature is confirmed as active in this version.

### 3. Why information is used
Information may be used to:
- respond to messages and enquiries;
- review, moderate and publish Reader's Corner contributions;
- receive and count poll votes and limit repeated voting;
- maintain, secure and troubleshoot the website;
- prevent spam, misuse and unauthorised access; and
- meet applicable legal obligations.

### 4. Reader's Corner and moderation
Reader's Corner submissions are subject to moderation. A submission may remain pending, be approved, rejected or deleted. Only approved feedback is intended to appear publicly. The public feedback retrieval function reviewed for this site returns the contributor's name, message and creation time; it does not return the email field.

Please do not include sensitive personal information, private contact details or information about another person in a public contribution.

### 5. Polls and voting information
Visitors can participate in polls without entering their name or email address. The voting system uses a browser-stored client token and a protected hash to recognise repeat votes for a poll. Poll results are presented as aggregate totals and percentages.

This design should be described as **pseudonymous voting**, not guaranteed complete anonymity. Technical information may also be processed by hosting and backend providers. See the separate Poll Privacy Notice in this package.

### 6. Sharing and service providers
Information may be processed by service providers needed to host and operate the website, including GitHub Pages and Supabase. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy documentation. Information is not intended to be sold. Access to private contact and feedback records should be limited to authorised administrators and service personnel who need it.

### 7. Retention
Information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, security and provider requirements. The operator's proposed review periods and deletion workflow are set out in the internal Data Protection Operations Manual. Actual database backups, logs and provider retention periods still need verification.

### 8. Security
The site uses Supabase Row Level Security and controlled database functions for certain submissions and public data retrieval. A technical review identified several positive controls, but some ownership, privilege, provider-retention and live-access checks remain outstanding. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

### 9. Children
The website may be viewed by children. Public browsing does not require a visitor account. The operator should avoid unnecessary collection of children's personal data and should not introduce child registration, profiling, targeted advertising or additional data collection without a separate legal and safeguarding review and appropriate parent/guardian involvement.

### 10. Requests and grievances
For questions, correction or deletion requests, or privacy grievances, contact:

**Email:** tsudhakartsudhakar5@gmail.com

Please provide enough information to identify the relevant submission without sending passwords, identity documents or unnecessary sensitive information. The operator will review requests and respond as reasonably practicable, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-retention requirements.

### 11. External links
The website may link to book retailers, publishing platforms or other external websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

### 12. Changes
This policy may be updated when website features, providers or applicable requirements change. The version and effective date should be updated whenever a revised policy is published.
