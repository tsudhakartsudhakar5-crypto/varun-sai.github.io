THE BOOK

What if the biggest change you need is you?

The Time Changes is a philosophical and motivational exploration of how we grow — through self-awareness, discipline, courage, and the willingness to act even when we are unsure.

Across 21 chapters, it examines failure, purpose, and personal transformation without offering easy answers or exaggerated promises. It is an invitation to sit with time honestly, and to take the next step forward, one changed moment at a time.

Time will continue moving whether you change or not. The answer does not have to be perfect. Change is not something you prove with words. You prove it with the life you live after these pages are over.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Varun Sai

Varun Sai is the author of The Time Changes, a book born from personal reflection on discipline, failure, and growth. Through this work, he explores how ordinary moments of change — however small — shape who we become.

Writing is one of his ways of expressing his thoughts. He has also written Vimala Guna Krishna, a Shatakam dedicated to Lord Krishna, along with English quotes and an English poem titled The Guiding Soul, dedicated to a teacher who has guided and inspired him.

Outside writing, Varun Sai enjoys cricket and chess, exploring new things, and learning from different sources. He continues to see life as a journey of learning, growth, and change.
