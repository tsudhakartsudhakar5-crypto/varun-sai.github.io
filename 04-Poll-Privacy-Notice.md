# Poll Privacy Notice

You can participate in website polls without entering your name or email address.

To help prevent repeated voting, the website stores a browser-generated client token and uses a protected hash to recognise repeat votes for the same poll.

The voting system is designed not to store the raw client token in the `poll_votes` table or publicly display individual voter identities.

Poll results are displayed as aggregate totals and percentages.

However, the use of a browser token and associated technical records means that poll participation should not be described as completely anonymous. Technical information may also be processed by the website's hosting and backend providers.

Please do not submit personal or sensitive information through poll features.
